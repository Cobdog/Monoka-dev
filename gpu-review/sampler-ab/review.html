THE RULE Euler (combined eval): x' = (s_next/s) · x + (1 − s_next/s) · x0 [ODE step] DMD re-noise (DMAD's own): x0 = x + s · velocity; x' = (1 − s_next) · x0 + s_next · FRESH noise Both arms use the SAME sigma schedule: simple/4 @ shift 12 (video) / 2 or 3 (audio) — DMAD's own sigma_grid(4,12) is knot-identical to our card grid; only the step rule differs. On this engine the packed video+audio latent is carried as a single-schedule flow latent, and the uniform re-noise over the pack is EXACTLY DMAD's per-stream rule (audio re-noised at its own shifted sigma) — verified algebraically + numerically to 1e-16 before the run. CANARY GATES (39f pre-arms): re-noise ENGAGED (differs from Euler bit-wise), DETERMINISTIC (duplicate bit-identical through a cache-bust alias), knots == card grid, RMS finite, audio non-silent.
